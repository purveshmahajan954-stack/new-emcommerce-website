Our Story

Why
Rituals

Makhana has been part of Indian homes for generations — roasted in small batches, eaten as an evening habit, never marketed as anything more than a simple snack.

We started Rituals to change that.

Working closely with nutrition research on cognitive health, we found that makhana carries properties long associated with focus and mental clarity — properties that got lost somewhere between tradition and mass production.

So we went back to basics. Every batch is roasted, never fried, to protect its natural nutrient profile. No shortcuts, no fillers — just makhana, done right.

Rituals is led by the Mathur family: Anjna Mathur, Founder; Arun Mathur, CEO; and Ayush Mathur, MD — three generations, one product philosophy.

We're not reinventing makhana. We're just giving it the credit it deserves.
