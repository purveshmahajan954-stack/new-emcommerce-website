Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rituals Makhana completely fried or roasted? +
Rituals Makhana is 100% slow-roasted without deep frying. We use a tiny drizzle of cold-pressed oil only to allow our natural spices and Himalayan pink salt to adhere evenly to every lotus seed.
What is the shelf life and how should I store it? +
Every pouch has a shelf life of 9 months from the date of manufacturing. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Reseal the pouch tightly after opening to preserve the signature crunch.
Is it suitable for keto, vegan, and gluten-free diets? +
Yes! Makhana is naturally gluten-free, plant-based, and low in glycemic index. It is an ideal clean snack for weight management, fasting rituals, and health-conscious lifestyles.
