About Us

We are not a snack brand.
We are a performance brand.

Born in Vadodara. Built for the era of infinite distraction. Engineered for the high-achiever who refuses to compromise.

01

Fuel for the Cognitive Mind

We live in an era of infinite distraction. Every day, we are bombarded by cheap, high-sugar, low-nutrition "snack fuel" designed to distract us, not drive us. We've all felt it: the 3:00 PM wall, the sluggishness after a heavy meal, the brain fog that sets in when we need to be at our sharpest. For the high achiever—the creator, the entrepreneur, the student, the executive—this isn't just an inconvenience. It is a barrier to greatness.

Our story began in Vadodara with a simple question: Why does the food we consume to fuel our work day often end up working against us? We were tired of the compromise. We wanted a snack that could keep up with the pace of our ambition—something that didn't just fill a gap, but fueled a state of mind. We looked at the ancient tradition of the Makhana (Foxnut) and realized it was the perfect vessel. But we knew that to make it fit for the modern high-performer, we had to re-engineer the entire process.

03

The World's First Cognitive Performance Snack

We built Rituals to be more than just a snack; it is a performance tool. We are the first brand in India to introduce the "Cognitive Performance" category. We don't fry our ingredients; we slow-roast them using proprietary techniques that preserve the natural integrity of the superfood. We've meticulously balanced our seasoning profiles to be clean, sharp, and sophisticated.

We don't talk about calories or weight loss. We talk about Cognitive Vitality. Whether you are locking into a deep-work sprint, preparing for a high-stakes presentation, or navigating a complex creative project, Rituals is engineered to be your performance-partner.
